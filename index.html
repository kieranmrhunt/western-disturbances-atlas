Data, definitions and reproducibility

The catalogue grain, upper-level diagnostic and regional-impact definitions are kept explicit.

Catalogue

WD v5 atlas snapshot contains 16,850 trajectories and 398,584 three-hourly track fixes from January 1950 through December 2025.

Scope

Positions and diagnostics are derived from ERA5. The atlas is a research catalogue, not an operational warning service or an official best track.

Event identity

track_id identifies one Lagrangian trajectory. Genesis is its first published fix and lysis its last. Display names use genesis year and within-year genesis order, for example WD 2017 042; exports retain the catalogue ID as well.

WD-specific physics

  • Upper-tropospheric vorticity: track-centred relative vorticity averaged through the 450–300 hPa layer and spectrally truncated to T42, reported in 10⁻⁵ s⁻¹.
  • 24 h precipitation: track-centred and regional accumulated precipitation, reported in mm.
  • Path length: great-circle distance summed between consecutive three-hourly fixes.
  • Percentiles: fixed against all 16,850 catalogue trajectories; filters do not rescale them.

The atlas intentionally omits LPS-specific pressure-deficit classes, IBTrACS matching, BSISO filters and Indian-state precipitation fills.

Regions

Genesis regions reproduce the four-cluster method behind Figure 5 of the 2025 WD review: North Atlantic jet stream, Alps/Northern Europe, Mediterranean and Zagros. Classification uses the original winter-catalogue k-means centres in the transformed longitude–latitude space; points beyond the 99.5% within-cluster distance envelope are labelled Other.

Precipitation-impact regions: the five boxes are Karakoram (74–78°E, 35–37.5°N), Hindu Kush (68–73°E, 34–37°N), Western Himalaya (73–79°E, 30–34°N), Central Himalaya (79–86°E, 27–30.5°N), and north India (74–85°E, 25–30°N). “Dominant” identifies the box with the largest peak 24 h precipitation for that trajectory. Boxes overlap and are transparent analytical conventions, not administrative boundaries.

Map and weather fields

Density counts each track at most once per one-degree cell, but remains selectable through the underlying trajectories. The time slider follows the selected track’s three-hourly centres. Weather backgrounds are independent ERA5 context fields: 350-hPa relative vorticity at 0.5° and trailing 24 h precipitation at 1°. They do not replace the track-centred catalogue diagnostics.
