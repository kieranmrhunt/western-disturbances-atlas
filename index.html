Data, definitions and reproducibility

The catalogue grain, upper-level diagnostic and regional-impact definitions are kept explicit.

Catalogue and time

WD v6 contains 16,298 trajectories and 460,411 three-hourly track fixes from January 1950 through December 2025.

track_id identifies one Lagrangian trajectory. Genesis is its first published fix and lysis its last. Display names use genesis year and within-year genesis order, for example WD 2017 042; exports retain the catalogue ID.

Winter year assigns December to the following year. A meridian crossing is the first linearly interpolated crossing of the selected longitude; trajectories and charts remain on their original clocks.

Positions and diagnostics are derived from ERA5. This is a research catalogue, not an operational warning service or official best track.

WD-specific physics

  • Upper-tropospheric vorticity: track-centred relative vorticity averaged through the 450–300 hPa layer and spectrally truncated to T42, reported in 10⁻⁵ s⁻¹.
  • 24 h precipitation: track-centred and regional accumulated precipitation, reported in mm.
  • Path length: great-circle distance summed between consecutive observed fixes.
  • Percentiles: fixed against all 16,298 catalogue trajectories; filters do not rescale them.

The evolution selectors expose the core series plus 56 lazy ERA5 diagnostics. Up to three line variables use separate axes while precipitation bars remain visible. The vertical panel combines the 850, 700 and 500 hPa track-centred fields.

The atlas intentionally omits LPS-specific pressure-deficit classes, IBTrACS matching, BSISO filters and Indian-state precipitation fills.

Regions, routes and sequences

Genesis regions reproduce the four-cluster method behind Figure 5 of the 2025 WD review. Classification uses its transformed longitude–latitude cluster centres; points outside the original 99.5% distance envelopes are Other.

Route archetypes are an exploratory eight-cluster k-means classification of longitude and latitude interpolated at nine common elapsed-life fractions. The labels describe cluster medians and are not meteorological event classes.

Multi-WD spells link systems within the same winter and dominant precipitation-impact region when the next genesis is no more than 72 h after the latest lysis. The five impact boxes overlap and are analytical conventions, not administrative boundaries.

Analogues prioritise standardised full-trajectory shape, with smaller penalties for genesis month, lifetime, intensity and precipitation.

Maps and impact context

Density counts each track at most once per one-degree cell, but remains selectable through the underlying trajectories. The slider and selected-system chart both move the track fix and contemporaneous weather frame.

Independent ERA5 weather backgrounds provide positive 350-hPa vorticity, trailing 24 h precipitation, 500-hPa wind speed, temperature and specific humidity, and mean-sea-level pressure. A staged field is enabled only after its complete 1950–2025 archive passes validation.

The selected-system footprint accumulates hourly ERA5 precipitation from published genesis through lysis on a 1° grid. It provides spatial context but does not partition liquid and solid precipitation or estimate impacts.

Jet and circulation context

The jet relationship uses the daily latitude and speed of the maximum 200-hPa wind between 20 and 55°N at the WD longitude from 50 to 80°E. Thirty-four WD fixes on 29–31 October 2023 are absent from the dedicated source and remain unavailable.

Genesis-month NOAA PSL ONI, NAO, AO and PNA values use ±0.5 category thresholds. Daily MJO phase comes from the Bureau of Meteorology RMM index; amplitude below 1 is weak. These filters describe contemporaneous regimes and do not imply causation.
